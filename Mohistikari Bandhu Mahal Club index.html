About Our Club

Preserving Tradition Since 1987

Welcome to the official website of the Mohistikari Bandhu Mahal Tapasili Club. Established in 1987, our club has been a cornerstone of cultural and social harmony in our community. We are officially registered under the Government of West Bengal with Registration Number S/IL/No-73100.

Every year, we organize a grand Kali Puja, bringing together devotees and community members for days of devotion, joy, and celebration.
